# Fishing equipment — second visual pass

Four revised models: fish oil screw press, fishing net rack, fish dryer and eel rack.

- End-grain checks and varied weathered structural timber.
- Proud rack tenons, locking wedges and tucked lashing ends.
- Riveted press hoop splices, retained feed paddle and a grounded working rope coil.
- Bound cork floats, a peg-supported net mending shuttle and irregular rope hanks.
- Fish gill creases and pectoral fins; eel dorsal ribbons and mouth detail.

The v1 models and renders remain as the earlier review baseline. Press input/output and animation markers are preserved.

Each model has an editable Blender file, GLB export and fresh studio render. Geometry checks passed for finite coordinates, nonempty meshes, recorded bounds and triangle counts. See `geometry-checks.json`.

Static visual review only. Runtime registration, collision, native shader matching, animation and in-game acceptance remain pending. No local installation or public publishing was performed for this pass.
