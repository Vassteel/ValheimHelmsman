# Harbor models — second visual pass

Eight revised models: keel cradle, launching rollers, framing gantry, two cranes and three pulley sets.

- Weathered timber with shallow end-grain checks and subtle corner variation.
- Pegged and wedged gantry joints, crane pivot cheeks and through axles.
- Stitched cradle pads and strapped roller bearings.
- Irregular layered rope coils with cord bindings, knots and loose working ends.
- Tighter windlass rope winding, curved lifting slings, pulley washers and retaining pins.

The v2 models and renders remain as the earlier review baseline. Each crane retains six snap markers.

Each model has an editable Blender file, GLB export and fresh studio render. Geometry checks passed for finite coordinates, nonempty meshes, recorded bounds and triangle counts. See `geometry-checks.json`.

Static visual review only. Runtime registration, collision, native shader matching, animation and in-game acceptance remain pending. No local installation or public publishing was performed for this pass.
