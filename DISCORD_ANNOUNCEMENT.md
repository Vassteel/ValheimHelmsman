⚓ **Valheim stýrimaður (Valheim Helmsman) — IN TESTING**

A little gull with a big job: helping you sail between your own named docks.

Helmsman is an experimental ship autopilot built around player-made ports. Place a **Dock Ward**, name your destination, position its ship ghost, and speak to the guide gull to plan a voyage.

**What the prototype includes:**
• Named docks with adjustable arrival position and approach direction — no ship needed to configure them.
• A ghost preview for planning tight berths, plus **reverse departure** to back out before turning toward your destination.
• A boarding countdown so you have time to hop aboard.
• Wind-aware sailing and paddling, with water-route planning and obstacle/depth checks intended to help avoid running aground.
• An onboard gull menu to stop or change destination after departure, plus manual helm takeover whenever you need it.
• A slow arrival, release of ship control, and a gull that flies away when its job is done.
• A dedicated **Helmsman** build category and menus styled to match Quartermaster.

🧪 **Current scope: solo worlds and the vanilla Karve only.** This is an early testing build, not a finished release. Dock handling, route finding, and sailing behavior still need in-game validation; use a disposable test world and stay ready to take the helm.

Testing feedback is especially useful for dock setup, backing out, boarding, wind changes, and shoreline navigation. Share issues in **bug-reports** and ideas in **suggestions**.
